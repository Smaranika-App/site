Smaranika Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Developer: Smaranika, an independent app
Contact: privacy@smaranika.app

Smaranika ("the app") helps you pick the best photos from your own Photos library, organize them by place and event, and turn them into shareable pages, books and postcards. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your photos stay on your phone. Smaranika has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect data that identifies you, and we do not sell or share it.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There is no account or sign-in. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and we run no server that receives your data. We do not receive your photos, your locations, your captions or anything else you create in the app, and we do not sell or share personal information. The only information that leaves your device is the place and update-check information sent to the services described under 'Services the app contacts' (Apple for place names and maps, Expo for update checks, and TestFlight for beta testers), and we do not use it to identify or track you.

Your photos and Photos library

The app asks for access to your Photos library so it can read your photos and their dates and locations. You can allow access to all photos or to selected photos only.

  • The app only reads your photos. It never changes, moves, deletes or adds to your Photos library.
  • Photos are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

Your collections, the photos you keep, captions and settings are saved on your phone, inside the app. Deleting the app removes this data from your device. Backup files are covered under Exports and sharing.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple's device backup may include this app's data, including copies of photos the app stores. Deleting the app removes the data from your device; it does not delete an existing device backup.

Services the app contacts

Some features need the internet. The app contacts only the three services below, and none of them sends your photos anywhere.

  1. Place names and maps (Apple). To turn a photo's location into a place name (for example, "Reykjavik"), to look up a place you type in, and to show maps, the app uses Apple's location and mapping services built into iOS. Only the coordinates or place text needed for each request are sent to Apple, which handles them under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  2. App updates (Expo). The app checks for and downloads updates to its own code from Expo's update service (EAS Update, operated by Expo). This shares technical details with Expo: your device operating system, the app's project ID and a random update token (and the IP address that comes with any web request). Expo acts as a processor for this data. No photos, locations, captions or other content you create are sent. See Expo's Privacy Policy.
  3. TestFlight (Apple), beta testers only. If you install the app through TestFlight, Apple may collect feedback and crash information that you choose to share, under Apple's terms.

Exports and sharing

When you export a PDF, image, postcard or backup file, it is created on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share or save it, for example with AirDrop, Messages or Save to Files. After that, the app has no control over where the file goes. A backup file contains the locations, captions and file names of your photos and is not encrypted, so save or share it only somewhere you trust.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information that identifies anyone.

Your choices

You can change or revoke Photos access at any time in iOS Settings > Smaranika. Because we do not hold any data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or export on your behalf; deleting the app removes the app's data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
